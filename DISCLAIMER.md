# Disclaimer

This project is for personal education and curiosity. It is **not a medical device
and not medical advice**, and it has not been clinically validated.

- Consumer genotyping chips test a small, fixed set of positions. They miss most disease-causing
  variants, and their rare-variant calls have high false-positive rates. A "not detected" result
  rarely rules anything out.
- Imputed genotypes are statistical estimates, not measurements.
- Polygenic scores describe small shifts in population-level risk, were mostly developed in people
  of European ancestry, and say little about any one person.
- Pharmacogenomic results here cover only some alleles of each gene. Never start, stop or change a
  medication based on them. Ask a clinician to order a clinical pharmacogenomic test.
- Ancestry estimates depend on the reference panels used and can be confidently wrong for
  under-represented populations.

Discuss anything health-related with a doctor or genetic counselor, and confirm it with a clinical
(CLIA/CAP-accredited or equivalent) test before acting on it.

Analyzing relatives' DNA reveals things about them. Get each person's consent before including
their data, and before sharing any page that shows their results.
